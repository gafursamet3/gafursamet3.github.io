Design and Analysis of RL and RC High-Pass Filters

This project focused on designing, simulating, and evaluating RL and RC high-pass filters to achieve a specific cut-off frequency of 1.6 kHz.

Simulation & Measurement

Theoretical filter behavior was modeled using LTspice to conduct transient and AC sweep analyses across a frequency range of 100 Hz to 100 kHz. Physical components were measured using an LCR meter to determine actual resistance, inductance, and capacitance values, allowing for accurate error calculation between theoretical and real-world cut-off frequencies.

Frequency Response & Load Analysis

I generated comprehensive Bode plots using MATLAB to compare the magnitude and phase responses of both filter types. The project also included designing a modified RC high-pass filter to meet specific input impedance constraints, analyzing how the introduction of a load resistor alters the circuit's equivalent resistance and shifts the cut-off frequency.
